03 — Icons

Icons

A custom line-icon set drawn inline as SVG. Every glyph follows the same stroke rules — uniform weight, consistent corners, no decorative flourishes.

03.0

Drawing rules

Format
Inline SVG with fill="none"
Stroke
1.5px (1.6 – 1.8 for tab bar and small UI)
Linecap
round — never butt or square
Linejoin
round
Color
currentColor — inherits parent text color or category tint
Canvas
24 × 24 viewBox · rendered at 14 – 22px
