Step 2 of 3 Budgets

What do you spend money on?

Budgets are pools you fund and spend from — think of them as envelopes for each kind of expense.

Food
groceries, meals, chai
Transport
rickshaw, fuel, ride-share
Fun
going out, movies, games
Subscriptions
netflix, spotify, gym
Budgets start at zero. You'll move money into them from your bank or cash when you're ready — that's how you decide what to spend on each.
