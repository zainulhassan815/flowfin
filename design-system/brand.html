00 — Brand

Brand.

A wordmark that ends in a small cream period — a quiet declarative sentence, not a slogan. The mark, its companion monogram, the app icon, and the rules that hold them together.

flowfin
Wordmark · Primary

Set in Literata italic at weight 300, the wordmark whispers rather than shouts. The cream period is its own word: a quiet stop. It reads as "flowfin." — full stop, not "flowfin!"

00.6

Voice & tone

How FlowFin speaks — quiet, declarative, never moralizing about money. It surfaces facts, lets the reader draw conclusions, and uses italic only when the prose itself wants emphasis.

The four axes
Quiet
not loud

One number, one verb. No exclamations, no emoji, no "you've got this!". The interface should feel like a librarian, not a coach.

Declarative
not chatty

State the fact. "Rent is due in three days." Not "Looks like rent's coming up soon — wanted to give you a heads up!"

Honest
not cheerful

A bad number is a bad number. We don't dress it up or apologize for it. Empty states say "Nothing here yet" — not "Adventure awaits!"

Specific
not generic

"Spent Rs 6,000 on food" beats "Some spending happened." Numbers, dates, categories — never "items" or "things".

Copy in practice
Say Rent is due in 3 days.
Avoid ⚠️ Heads up! Don't forget — rent's coming up soon!
Say You've spent Rs 6,000 on food. That's 40% of this month's budget.
Avoid Whoa! Food spending is getting wild this month 🍔
Say Nothing here yet. Add a recurring to get started.
Avoid Looks a little empty in here! Let's add your first recurring ✨
Say Reset all data. Permanent · cannot be undone.
Avoid Uh oh! Are you sure you want to do this? This is super risky!
00.7

Personality

Three words to test design and copy against. If a decision doesn't pass all three, reconsider it.

01
Editorial

Reads like a printed weekly — generous margins, restrained color, a serif holding the long-form, mono holding the numbers.

02
Attentive

Notices the small things — that you paid rent late, that subscriptions crept up, that this Sunday was quieter than usual.

03
Unhurried

Never urgent. Animations are short and functional. Charts don't dance. The user is the one in charge of pace.
